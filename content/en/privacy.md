## Browsing this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking technologies. No personal information is collected simply by visiting it.

## Contact form

If you choose to use the contact form, the information you provide is sent to Netlify Forms and is used only to read and respond to your message. This may include your name, email address, company or project details, budget, and message.

Form submissions are stored by Netlify. For more information, see [Netlify's Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

## Retention and your choices

Contact information is kept only for as long as needed to respond to a message or continue a conversation you started. You can ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted by emailing [hello@evelyn-perez.com](mailto:hello@evelyn-perez.com).

## External links

This website includes links to external services such as LinkedIn and GitHub. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
