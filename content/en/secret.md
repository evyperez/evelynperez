I’m the youngest of three sisters. I spent my childhood moving from city to city until my dad was transferred to São Paulo, where I lived for many years.

As a teenager, I went to a boarding school in the countryside of São Paulo state. I made friends there who are still in my life today and mean as much to me as family.

I’m used to change, and I think that also played a part in my decision to go into design. I like variety and working on different things.

I worked on graphic design projects, including print materials and books. Then I started customizing websites and thought: programming sounds fun. I started learning frontend development and, voilà, I’ve been a developer ever since.

In 2023, I got a job offer in Portugal and decided to take a chance. The language is the same, but the vocabulary isn’t. Just when I thought I was getting the hang of understanding people here, I spoke to someone from Alentejo and realized I’d been a little too confident, haha. Those differences in language are what made me add a European Portuguese version of this site.

I still like writing code by hand. AI is here to make our work easier, but every now and then I like being a little rebellious.

I also enjoy getting to know other cultures, reading, playing games and writing, as you’ve probably noticed. This was supposed to be a short introduction, but I think I got a little carried away, haha.
