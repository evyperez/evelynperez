[YouBet! School](https://youbetschool.com/en/) helps students study abroad. I helped establish the company and was responsible for its visual identity, stationery and company website.

I still occasionally help with brand materials and website updates.

## Development

I chose Hugo because the website did not need to connect to an API and its content could be presented as static pages. This kept development simple, without the need for a more complex framework.

The code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/evyperez/youbet).
