Voto Legal is a platform for individual donations to election campaigns, created by the Movement Against Electoral Corruption (MCCE) and AppCívico after Brazil banned corporate donations. The aim was to make donations more transparent by recording them on a blockchain.

In 2018, Voto Legal won first place at the 15th Innovare Award, in the Justice and Citizenship category.

I helped develop the main website and the donation page, both built with Vue.js. It was the first project where I was responsible for developing some pages on my own.

## Development

The home page introduced the platform. Each campaign page let people make a donation and view the amount raised and the list of contributions received.

My mentor was Sobral, who guided me during those early days and is still one of the best front-end developers I have worked with.

## My experience at AppCívico

[AppCívico](https://appcivico.com) is a company focused on projects with a positive impact on society. It was my first job as a programmer, from 2017 to 2018. Before that, I had done some freelance customization work.

That was where I started building interfaces from scratch, alongside other developers. Accessibility was part of the work from the start. That experience gave me my foundation in interface development and accessibility.
